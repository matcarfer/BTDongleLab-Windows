# Privacy

[Español](PRIVACY.es.md)

BTDongleLab does not collect or send data. It does not use the Internet and has no ads or analytics.

- **Reads:** Bluetooth, USB and driver data, and the Wi-Fi of the same device.
- **Saves:** one `.html` and one `.log` per dongle, in the program folder. **They include the MAC address, the PC's Bluetooth name, serial numbers and device identifiers.** Check them before sharing.
- **Deep analysis:** records a temporary Bluetooth trace for a few seconds and deletes it when done.
- Links open only if you click them.
